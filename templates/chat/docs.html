Documentation

Welcome to the Shy2Ask documentation. Our platform allows you to bridge the gap between shyness and action by relaying your questions anonymously.

How it Works

Shy2Ask acts as a trusted intermediary. When you submit a request, we:

  • Validate: Ensure the request is lawful and respectful.
  • Relay: Contact the recipient via your chosen method (Email, Letter, or Phone).
  • Maintain Anonymity: Your identity is never revealed to the recipient.
  • Facilitate Communication: Relay responses back to you through our secure platform.

WebSockets (WSS)

Real-time updates are handled via WebSockets (WSS).

WSS endpoint: wss://backend.shy2ask.com/ws/chat/{request_id}/

Security & Privacy

Privacy is our core value. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We strictly adhere to Swiss data protection standards.
